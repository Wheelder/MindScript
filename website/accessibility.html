Accessibility Statement

We are working to make wheelder.com and all sub-sites accessible. This page is updated as we identify and fix issues.

Conformance target

Our target is WCAG 2.1 Level AA for MindScript public pages.

Known limitations

No known major limitations at this time. Some older generated pages may still need manual review as content and UI components change.

Assistive technology testing

Keyboard navigation and automated checks are reviewed during site updates. Full NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver testing has not yet been completed for every sub-site.

Contact

Report accessibility issues at accessibility@wheelder.com or support@wheelder.com.
