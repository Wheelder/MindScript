Frequently asked

Is there a free trial?

14-day free trial on all paid plans. No credit card required to start the trial - billing begins automatically when the trial ends, and you can cancel any time before then.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade instantly. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Annual plans are pro-rated.

Do you offer discounts?

Annual billing is 20% off the monthly rate. Verified non-profits, students and academic researchers can apply for additional discounts via hello@wheelder.com.

How does support work?

All paid plans include email support during business hours. Business and Enterprise tiers include Slack/Teams shared channels, priority response SLAs and named contacts.

Where is data stored?

EU (Frankfurt) and US (Virginia) by default on Business+ plans. Custom regions and on-prem options available on Enterprise.
